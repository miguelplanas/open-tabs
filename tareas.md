Responsive score adaptation: The application should detect the device (mobile, tablet, or computer) and automatically adjust the score width to the screen width. The goal is to eliminate horizontal scrolling, as having to drag your finger to see the rest of the page is very inconvenient while playing guitar.

Auto-scroll speed control and reduction: The default auto-scroll speed should be much slower. Furthermore, speed control should be improved: the slider should have fixed values ​​(divided into 10 or 20 steps or "segments") and a text field should be included to directly enter the desired speed.

Auto-scroll starting margin: The beginning of the score should be rendered slightly lower on the screen (add a top margin). This way, when auto-scroll begins, reading and following the notes will feel much more natural for the user.

Final limit of auto-scroll: Auto-scroll should stop exactly at the end of the last line of the score. The screen should not continue scrolling down and displaying unnecessary white space after the song has finished.
